# Business Intelligence Tools

## 🧠 Specialized Skills

### Market Research
- **`company-research`**: (Exa) Comprehensive deep dive into companies. Findings products, news, competitors, and financials.
- **`web-search-advanced-financial-report`**: (Exa) Locate 10-Ks, earnings reports, and other financial documentation.

### Networking & People
- **`people-research`**: (Exa) Find professional profiles, bios, and background info on individuals.
- **`web-search-advanced-tweet`**: Monitor brand sentiment or industry news on X.

### Strategic Analysis
- **`critical-analysis`**: Evaluate business plans and market claims.
- **`lateral-thinking`**: Brainstorm marketing strategies or pivot ideas.
- **`grant-proposal`**: Useful for startup funding applications.

## 🛠️ Google Workspace Integration
- **Gmail**: Draft outreach emails or manage correspondence.
- **Calendar**: Schedule meetings.
- **Sheets**: Financial modeling and data analysis.
- **Docs**: Business plan writing.
