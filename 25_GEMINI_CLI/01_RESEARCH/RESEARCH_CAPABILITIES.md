# Research Capabilities

## 🧠 Specialized Skills
These skills are powered by the **Co-Researcher** extension, designed for PhD-level academic and professional research.

### Methodology & Planning
- **`research-manager`**: Orchestrates complex research projects.
- **`research-methodology`**: Design study protocols, sampling strategies, and controls.
- **`ethics-review`**: Analyze ethical implications and prepare IRB documentation.
- **`grant-proposal`**: Structure and write research grant applications.

### Review & Synthesis
- **`systematic-review`**: PRISMA-compliant systematic reviews.
- **`literature-review`**: Deep dives into existing academic literature.
- **`research-synthesis`**: Merging multiple sources into a coherent narrative.
- **`peer-review`**: Critical evaluation of manuscripts.

### Analysis
- **`critical-analysis`**: Logic checking and fallacy identification.
- **`quantitative-analysis`**: Statistical test selection and power analysis.
- **`qualitative-analysis`**: Thematic analysis and coding schemes.
- **`hypothesis-testing`**: Formal hypothesis formulation.

### Investigation
- **`multi-source-investigation`**: Fact-checking across diverse/conflicting sources.
- **`lateral-thinking`**: Generating novel connections.

## 🔎 Advanced Search Tools (Exa)
- **`web-search-advanced-research-paper`**: Dedicated academic paper search.
- **`web-search-advanced-tweet`**: Social sentiment and real-time info.
- **`web-search-advanced-personal-site`**: Niche expert blogs.

## 🚀 Deep Research Agent
- **`research_start`**: Initiates a background agent to conduct deep internet research on a topic and generate a comprehensive report.
- **File Search (RAG)**: Ground research in your own uploaded PDFs/documents.
